DailyMate

Tasks & Habits · Plan your day, build habits

DailyMate is a warm, gentle companion for your tasks and habits — it walks you through the day without pressure. Plan what matters, build the habits you care about, and focus in calm, private surroundings.

What it does

  • Time-slotted tasks with gentle reminders
  • Goals and habits with progress rings and a calendar
  • A Pomodoro focus timer with Live Activity
  • A countdown board for the dates that matter
  • Home-screen and Lock-Screen widgets
  • Reports, insights, and rewards

Private by design

DailyMate is local-first. Your tasks, notes, and progress stay on your device — there's no account to create and nothing is sent to our servers. A private iCloud backup keeps everything safe, for free.
